# Compliance & Responsible Automation

## Product rule

The platform must not be designed to bypass:

- rate limits
- authentication
- anti-bot controls
- platform restrictions
- privacy controls

## Data minimization

Store only what is needed.

## Suppression

A do-not-contact record must prevent future outreach.

## UK-focused readiness

Because the initial market may include UK businesses, build support for:

- privacy notice
- retention
- deletion
- suppression
- data-subject workflows
- processor/vendor records

Obtain legal advice for actual lawful-basis/contacting rules before production campaigns.

## Provider-specific compliance

Treat Google, Meta/Instagram and WhatsApp rules as separate integration contracts.
