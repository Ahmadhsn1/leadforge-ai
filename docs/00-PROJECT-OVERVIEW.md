# LeadForge AI — Project Overview

## Purpose

LeadForge AI is a B2B SaaS platform for prospect discovery, verification, business intelligence, personalized outreach preparation, conversation intelligence, and CRM.

## Core promise

Find businesses worth contacting, prove why they are worth contacting, prepare the right message, track the conversation, and learn from outcomes.

## Core pipeline

Discovery → Normalization → Verification → Enrichment → Evidence → AI Intelligence → Scoring → Personalization → Outreach Queue → Conversation Intelligence → CRM → Analytics

## Initial target users

- Web/design agencies
- Marketing agencies
- Lead-generation agencies
- Freelancers
- Appointment setters
- Small sales teams

## Initial internal customer

TractionX Digital.

## V1 focus

Google Places/authorized discovery + verification + enrichment + AI intelligence + lead scoring + personalized drafts + CRM/export.

## Later

Supported WhatsApp/Instagram/email integrations, follow-up automation, AI sales copilot, billing, agency workspaces, white label.

## Product principles

1. Evidence before claims.
2. Async jobs for long-running work.
3. Provider-agnostic AI.
4. Multi-tenant from day one.
5. Idempotent workers.
6. Human control before autonomous outreach.
7. Compliance is an architecture concern, not a final checklist.
