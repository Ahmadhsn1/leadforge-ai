# Evidence Layer

## Why this exists

The AI must not invent business pain points.

## Evidence object

Each evidence item contains:

- what was observed
- source
- source URL/reference
- observed_at
- confidence
- optional expiration

## Example

Statement: No dedicated website detected.
Source: authorized business listing + website resolution.
Confidence: 0.98.

## AI contract

AI receives evidence IDs and facts. AI may:

- summarize
- infer a clearly labeled opportunity
- rank supported issues

AI may not:

- invent metrics
- claim hidden problems
- fabricate conversations
- state unsupported revenue loss
