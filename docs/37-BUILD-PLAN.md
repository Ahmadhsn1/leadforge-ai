# Build Plan

## Phase 0 — Foundation

Monorepo, TypeScript, auth, organizations, database, Redis, queue, CI.

## Phase 1 — Campaign + discovery

Campaign builder, Places adapter, discovery workers, normalization, dedupe.

## Phase 2 — Verification + enrichment

Website checks, public contact/social enrichment, evidence.

## Phase 3 — AI intelligence

AI Gateway, OpenRouter, model routing, business analysis, pain points, scoring.

## Phase 4 — Personalization

Messages, channel-specific prompts, validation, follow-ups.

## Phase 5 — Outreach

Supported WhatsApp/Instagram/email integrations, webhooks, suppression, approval flow.

## Phase 6 — Copilot

Reply intelligence, suggested responses, next actions.

## Phase 7 — Analytics

Funnels, AI usage, performance.

## Phase 8 — SaaS

Plans, quotas, billing, team/agency features.

## Rule

Do not enable automated outreach before the intelligence and personalization pipeline is reliable.
