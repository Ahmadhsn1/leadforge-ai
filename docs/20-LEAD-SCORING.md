# Lead Scoring

## Goal

Rank prospects for human sales attention.

## Dimensions

Fit
Opportunity
Contactability
Maturity
Evidence confidence
Urgency/campaign fit

## Suggested weighting

Fit 25%
Opportunity 25%
Contactability 15%
Maturity 10%
Evidence confidence 15%
Urgency 10%

Weights should be configurable later.

## Output

0–100 score.

Buckets:
90–100 Hot
75–89 Warm
60–74 Moderate
<60 Low

## Explainability

The score must show dimension values and reasons.

## Calibration

Later, compare scores with actual replies/meetings/wins and recalibrate.
